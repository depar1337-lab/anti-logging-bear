              About and privacy

              Barry checks files with the same engine as the desktop app. The scan runs inside your browser, so your files never leave your device. The only time anything is sent is when you tap a VirusTotal link, which opens VirusTotal with a file's SHA-256 fingerprint.

              The web version can't watch your downloads folder, and it doesn't include the antivirus second opinion or the YARA rules. The Windows and Mac apps have both. See downloads.

              No scanner can prove a file is safe. "No threats found" means Barry didn't spot anything suspicious.
